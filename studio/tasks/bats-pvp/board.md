> Can be stolen: Plant or seed currently being carried from a biome. Cannot: Item already secured at your base; Plants growing in your garden; Money and permanent Speed; Index discoveries and completion rewards; Permanently owned plants or monsters in your inventory.
> Bats have a swing cooldown ... Hits require reasonable range and line of sight. Knockback is short enough that the game remains playable. Players cannot attack inside protected safe zones.
