# bats-pvp — brand pin

- Bat model: chunky low-poly bat about 4 studs long (handle, taper, barrel, cap). Wooden: `#B07A45` with a darker grip. Skins use the `IndexRewards` primary on the barrel and the secondary on the grip and cap; Neon accent only for glow skins (Lava, Crystal, Galaxy, Toxic, Divine Bloom).
- Impact: small white-yellow spark burst (built-in sparkles texture) plus a short "bonk" sound.
- Skin list: same row style as the shop menus (swatch, name, Equip or Equipped).
