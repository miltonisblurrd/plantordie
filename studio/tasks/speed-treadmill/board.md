## Original ask (quoted)

> Also add a treadmill ( See image of what it looks like): ... the treadmill isn't mainly about exercise or earning money. It's a way to build your Speed stat, which helps you travel faster, escape guardians while carrying stolen animals, and qualify for more advanced zones. Upgrading the treadmill increases the Speed earned from training, while equipped trails can multiply those gains. The personal treadmill is important because every player can train independently at their own base without competing for access to a shared machine.
>
> Every player gets a treadmill placed directly in front of their own house.
> Standing or running on it automatically generates Speed.
> Each player can upgrade their treadmill independently using their own money.
> Better treadmill levels generate more Speed per second.
> Equipped trails multiply Speed gains.
> Higher Speed helps players reach later biomes and escape guardians with valuable plants.
> Treadmill levels, Speed, and owned trails save when players leave.
