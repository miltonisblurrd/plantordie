- [ ] Digging dirt spots never starts a chase

- [ ] The RUN! banner stays up while the chase is active; the siren and flash are short only

- [ ] Where you get caught matters: near base is easy to recover, far away is harder

- [ ] Picking it up restarts the siren, red flash, RUN!, and the chase
- [ ] Caught again: the seed drops at the new spot, not the old one
- [ ] Timer runs out: the seed disappears permanently and can't be recovered
- [ ] Banking at your own base secures the seed and ends the chase

- [ ] Server validates chase start, catches, knockback, drop location, the timer, recovery, and banking

- [ ] RUN!, the warning effects, and the countdown work on PC, mobile, and tablet
